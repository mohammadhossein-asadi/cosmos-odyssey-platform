# Features

## Solar System Explorer
Interactive 3D visualization of all 8 planets with orbit mechanics, planet selection, and detailed information panels.

## Space Travel
Animated travel sequences between celestial destinations with warp effects and HUD overlay.

## Star Explorer
Interactive star map with constellation data, star filtering, and detailed star information.

## Encyclopedia
Searchable catalog of celestial objects with category filtering and detailed planet pages.

## Black Holes
Visualization of black hole phenomena including event horizon and accretion disk.

## Cosmic Timeline
Scrollable timeline of 13.8 billion years of cosmic history.

## Space Missions
Database of major space exploration missions with status tracking.

## Search
Global search with fuzzy matching across all data sources.

## User Profile
Exploration profile with achievement tracking system.
